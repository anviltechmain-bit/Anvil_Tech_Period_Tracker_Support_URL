Privacy Policy

Free Periods Tracker is designed as an offline period tracking app. The app stores your entries on your device. It does not use Firebase, developer-managed cloud sync, or third-party analytics SDKs for personal tracker records.

What the app stores

  • Period tracking entries, symptoms, sleep levels, and activity selections you save in the app. Older numeric sleep values are retained when upgrading.
  • Cycle settings and local app preferences needed to keep the app working.
  • The email address of the Google account you select for optional Drive backup. It is stored in app-private storage to show the connection and is removed when you disconnect Drive.
  • One optional private JSON backup file in your own Google Drive appDataFolder when you connect Google Drive backup.

Where data is stored

  • Saved locally inside app storage on the device, using Room database for tracker records.
  • Tracker records stay in Android app-private storage and are not written to shared device storage.
  • Device backup and cleartext traffic are disabled in the Android app configuration.
  • Before uploading, the app stages one JSON snapshot in app-private, backup-excluded storage. It is replaced by the next snapshot and removed on disconnect, clear app data, or uninstall.

Retention and deletion

  • Entries remain on the device until you edit them, clear app data, or uninstall the app. Empty record identifiers and timestamps may remain to stop cleared details from returning during sync.
  • Disconnecting stops this app from using Drive but does not delete the existing backup. You can remove its hidden app data using Google Drive settings. Older copies on another offline device can remain until that device next syncs; clearing this device does not remotely erase other devices.
  • The selected email is remembered if permission expires so you can reconnect. It is removed when you explicitly disconnect Google Drive from the app.
  • Connecting enables daily auto backup by default, unless you previously turned it off. You can change this at any time. Work is scheduled around 2:00 AM local time with internet required, but Android may delay it. An existing backup is protected until you review and confirm a merge restore.
  • The app developer does not keep a cloud copy of your cycle entries because the app does not sync them to developer servers.

What the app does not do

  • No app account creation; an existing Google account is needed only for optional Drive backup.
  • No developer-managed cloud sync.
  • No Firebase services.
  • No interstitial or other full-screen ads.
  • No third-party analytics or behavior tracking SDKs for personal health tracker data.

Banner ads

  • The app displays Google Mobile Ads banners below the Tracker week bar and above the Cycle overview.
  • The app does not display interstitial ads.
  • Period tracking entries are never sent to or used for advertising.
  • Google Mobile Ads may collect and share IP address, device and account identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
  • Where required, Google User Messaging Platform presents consent choices before banner requests. The app sends no tracker entries to Google Mobile Ads.
  • Accepting app terms is not advertising consent. Read Google's privacy policy for Google's processing.

Permissions and device behavior

  • Notifications are optional for the daily 8:00 PM reminder and automatic backup results. Alerts can appear on your lock screen; use Android notification settings to control visibility. Declining notifications does not block tracking or backup.
  • Google Drive backup uses the limited drive.appdata scope only, so the app does not request full Google Drive access.
  • The app does not request device storage permission and does not use a file picker or folder picker for backup and restore.
  • Where supported, open About > Manage ad privacy to revisit Google advertising consent choices.
  • Screenshots are intentionally allowed.
  • You can disconnect Google Drive from inside the app without deleting your local tracker data.

Private Drive backup and security

Google Drive backup is optional; offline tracking does not require a Google account. One JSON snapshot, anvil_free_periods_tracker_backup.json, is stored in your own private appDataFolder. It contains app/version metadata, record UUIDs and timestamps, tracker entries, and settings needed for restore. An older app_backup.json is adopted in place on a successful update rather than intentionally creating a second file. The developer does not receive, view, or store your backup or send tracker records to a developer server. Restore previews its date and record count before confirmation and merges without blindly wiping local data. Missing records are added; newer matching records are kept. Each later backup merges current cloud changes before uploading. Incomplete restores or detected conflicting cloud changes stop an upload instead of blindly overwriting the backup.

Google manages authorization. The app does not persist access or refresh tokens and does not log account emails or tracker records. Transfers use HTTPS and device files use Android app-private storage. The JSON backup is not separately end-to-end encrypted by this app; protection also depends on your device and Google account security. No storage permission, file picker, or full Drive access is requested. No security measure can guarantee protection against every compromise.

Connect and back up

  1. Open About > Settings > Cloud Backup, or the Cloud Backup card in History.
  2. Tap Connect Google Drive, choose your Google account, and allow the limited app-data access.
  3. If there is no backup, the app creates the first snapshot. If one exists, check its date and record count, then confirm a merge restore. Cancelling leaves it protected from upload.

Future Back Up Now actions use the remembered account without account selection when permission is valid. There is no file or folder selection. Internet and working Google Play services are needed for Drive; offline tracking still works without them.

Restore and automatic backup

Tap Restore, review the preview, and confirm the merge. The result shows imported, updated, skipped, and failed counts. Local-only entries stay. The same UUID is not imported twice; newer matching entries win. Independently created records for the same date combine their fields where possible instead of adding a duplicate day.

Daily auto backup defaults on after connecting unless previously disabled. It runs best-effort around 2:00 AM local time, with internet required; Android can delay it. Successful automatic backups are at least 20 hours apart. Manual backup and restore each have a 5-minute cooldown. This is snapshot backup with merge, not instant or continuous sync. Results are notified only if notifications are allowed. Check Last backup before changing devices.

If Drive needs attention

  • If permission expires, the status becomes Not connected but the account is remembered. Tap Connect or retry the action to authorize again. Background work never opens sign-in.
  • For a network error, check internet access and retry. A failed attempt does not use the successful-operation cooldown.
  • If an existing-backup review is required, use Restore and confirm the preview before trying to upload.
  • A sign-in configuration error, including code 10, needs a developer fix for the app package and signing certificate. Report the app version and installation source; do not send Google credentials.
  • If multiple backup files or a conflicting cloud update are reported, do not delete copies at random. Stop and contact support so existing data can be preserved.

Turn off or disconnect

Turn Auto backup off to stop daily work. Disconnect asks for confirmation, removes the local account link and staging snapshot, and turns auto backup off. It does not remove the Google account from your phone, delete local tracker entries, or delete the Drive backup. To delete the cloud copy, remove this app's hidden app data through Google Drive settings.

Health Notice

The educational content inside the app gives a general overview of menstrual phases and common symptoms. Bodies vary, and not everyone will have the same cycle length or experience the same symptoms.

  • Seek medical care for severe pain, very heavy bleeding, fainting, or sudden major cycle changes.
  • Ask a clinician if cycles stay shorter than 24 days or longer than 38 days over time.
  • Ask a clinician if periods stop for 90 days when not pregnant, breastfeeding, or under direct medical guidance.
